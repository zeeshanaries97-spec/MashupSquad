Platform Guidelines

TERMS OF SERVICE

Last Updated: November 2026 | MashupSquad Esports Arena

1. Nature of Platform (Skill-Based Esports)

MashupSquad Esports Arena (mashupsquad.vercel.app) is a competitive esports community platform organizing skill-based custom matches and scrims. All competitions hosted on our platform are strictly determined by player skill, strategy, and in-game performance—not chance.

2. Slot Registration & Eligibility

  • Players must submit accurate In-Game Names (IGN), Character IDs, and active WhatsApp contact details during slot reservation.
  • Slots are confirmed on a first-come, first-served basis once verification is completed via our official coordinator channel.
  • Sharing custom room IDs or passwords with unregistered players will result in immediate disqualification.

3. Platform Management & Service Fee

To ensure smooth tournament operations, custom room hosting, moderation, and community support, MashupSquad applies a transparent operational fee structure:

  • Service Allocation (Up to 20%): Up to 20% of total tournament registration contributions is retained by MashupSquad as a platform management and operational service fee.
  • Community Distribution (80% or more): The remaining 80% or more is dedicated directly toward event rewards and recognition for top-performing participants as announced in the official match briefing.
  • As the platform grows through live streaming and community sponsorships, MashupSquad aims to reduce or waive operational service fees in future events.

4. Fair Play & Anti-Cheat Policy

  • Any use of third-party hacks, scripts, teaming in solo lobbies, or unauthorized emulators (unless explicitly permitted for a specific lobby) is strictly prohibited.
  • Organizer decisions regarding match points, tie-breakers, and rule violations are final and binding to maintain fair competition.

5. Cancellations & Rescheduling

If an event is postponed or canceled by MashupSquad due to server maintenance or insufficient lobby slots, registered participants will have the option to transfer their slot to the next scheduled match or request a full return of their contribution through official support.

6. Contact & Support

For questions regarding these Terms of Service or event management, contact us at mashupsquads@gmail.com or WhatsApp +92 (0) 335 2808861 (Karachi, Pakistan).
