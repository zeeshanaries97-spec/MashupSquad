Fair Play Standards

ANTI-CHEAT POLICY

Last Updated: November 2026 | MashupSquad Esports Arena

1. Zero Tolerance for Cheating

MashupSquad Esports Arena is committed to fair, skill-based competition across all PUBG and Free Fire events. Any form of cheating, game tampering, or unfair advantage is strictly prohibited across all lobbies and matches.

2. Prohibited Practices

  • Hacks & Mods: Aimbot, ESP/wallhacks, no-recoil scripts, speed hacks, config edits, memory injectors, or modified game files.
  • Teaming & Collusion: Cooperating with opposing players or squads, intentionally feeding eliminations, or coordinating outside fair play.
  • Exploits & Stream Sniping: Abusing map/glitch exploits or watching active streams to gain live position information during a match.
  • Unauthorized Devices & Account Sharing: Using unapproved emulators/external triggers in mobile-only lobbies or playing on another registered player's slot.

3. Penalties & Strict No-Refund Policy for Cheaters

To protect honest players and uphold tournament integrity, strict disciplinary actions apply to any participant or team found violating fair play rules:

  • Immediate Disqualification: Any player or team caught cheating or violating fair play rules will be immediately disqualified from the ongoing match and entire tournament.
  • No Refund / Forfeiture of Slot Contribution: Players or teams disqualified for cheating, hacking, teaming, or rule violations will NOT receive any refund under any circumstances. Their registration contribution is strictly forfeited.
  • Forfeiture of Standings & Rewards: All points, kills, placements, and reward eligibility earned by the offending player or team will be completely canceled and reassigned to fair competitors.
  • Permanent Platform Ban: Confirmed cheaters will be permanently blacklisted from all future MashupSquad tournaments, scrims, and community lobbies.

4. Verification & Reporting

  • Match administrators review spectator footage, gameplay clips, and player reports before finalizing standings.
  • Players suspected of unusual gameplay may be asked to provide hand-cam or screen-recording proof upon coordinator request.
  • To report a suspected violation during a match, send match details and video/screenshot evidence to mashupsquads@gmail.com or WhatsApp +92 (0) 335 2808861.
