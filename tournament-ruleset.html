TOURNAMENT RULESET

Last Updated: November 2026 | MashupSquad Esports Arena

1. Supported Titles & Match Modes

To keep rules consistent across events, PUBG and Free Fire follow a unified competitive system across multiple squad formats and game modes:

  • Team Formats: Events may be hosted in Solo, Duo, or Squad formats depending on the tournament schedule.
  • Points-Based Modes (Classic / Battle Royale): Classic / BR matches use a standardized Kill + Survival/Placement points system regardless of total lobby or team count.
  • Direct Winner Modes (TDM / Clash Squad Style): TDM, Clash Squad (CS), or direct head-to-head/match-win formats do not use a points table—the match or bracket winner is declared as the Direct Winner.

2. Classic / BR Point System (PUBG & Free Fire)

In all Classic / Battle Royale matches (Solo, Duo, or Squad), total score is calculated using Elimination Points + Survival/Placement Points:

  • Elimination / Kill Points: 1 point per kill (applies to every confirmed elimination regardless of total teams in the lobby).
  • Survival / Placement Points: Awarded based on final placement/finish position as shown below (identical for both PUBG and Free Fire Classic/BR events).
Final Position / Rank Survival / Placement Points
1st Place (Winner) 10 Points
2nd Place 6 Points
3rd Place 5 Points
4th Place 4 Points
5th Place 3 Points
6th Place 2 Points
7th Place 1 Point

3. Direct Winner Format (TDM & CS Style)

  • In TDM (PUBG) or Clash Squad / CS (Free Fire) and similar direct match formats, no kill/placement points table is applied.
  • The winning player or team according to official match rules is recorded as the Direct Winner for that match or round.

4. Room Access, Punctuality & Tie-Breakers

  • Custom room credentials are shared before match start; players must join on time so lobbies start smoothly.
  • In points-based events, ties on total points are broken by total eliminations, highest single-match placement, or final match finish.
  • Players should keep clear end-of-match screenshots/results ready in case verification is required.

5. Event Updates & Support

Specific map settings, match counts, or lobby instructions for each event are shared through our official coordinator channel. For assistance, reach out at mashupsquads@gmail.com or WhatsApp +92 (0) 335 2808861.
