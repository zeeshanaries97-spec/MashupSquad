PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: November 2026 | MashupSquad Esports Arena

1. Introduction

Welcome to MashupSquad Esports Arena (mashupsquad.vercel.app). We operate a community-driven competitive gaming platform in Pakistan organizing skill-based custom esports scrims and tournaments. Your privacy is important to us, and this Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website or register for an event.

2. Information We Collect

When you reserve a tournament slot, subscribe to updates, or contact our support team, we may collect the following basic information:

  • Player Details: In-Game Name (IGN), Character ID / UID, and team or squad name.
  • Contact Information: WhatsApp phone number and email address for sending custom room IDs, passwords, and match schedules.
  • Technical & Analytics Data: Anonymous website traffic metrics (such as page views, device type, and browser information) via Vercel Analytics and Google Search Console to improve website performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

We strictly use your data for tournament operations and community communication:

  • To verify player registrations and assign match slots in custom lobbies.
  • To share custom room credentials, match timings, and rule updates via WhatsApp or email.
  • To maintain fair play, prevent cheating, and verify match results.
  • To send important announcements if you subscribe to our community newsletter.

4. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal contact information (such as your WhatsApp number or email address) to third parties or external marketers. Only your public In-Game Name (IGN) or match standings may be displayed on lobby rosters, live streams, or winner announcements.

We take reasonable administrative and technical precautions to keep your registration records secure and accessible only to authorized tournament coordinators.

5. Third-Party Services

Our platform is hosted on Vercel and may include links to official community channels such as WhatsApp, Discord, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok. Please note that MashupSquad is an independent community esports organizer and is not directly affiliated with Krafton, Tencent, Garena, or any game publisher.

6. Your Rights & Data Removal

You may request to update or delete your registration details or unsubscribe from tournament notifications at any time by reaching out to our support team.
